# Link Review

## Research topic

Personalization and concept editing of text-to-image diffusion models.

## Main references

- DreamBooth: Fine Tuning Text-to-Image Diffusion Models for Subject-Driven Generation.
- LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models.
- AlphaEdit: knowledge-preserving model editing methods.

## Project goal

Investigate methods for introducing new concepts into diffusion models while
minimizing unwanted changes to pretrained knowledge.
